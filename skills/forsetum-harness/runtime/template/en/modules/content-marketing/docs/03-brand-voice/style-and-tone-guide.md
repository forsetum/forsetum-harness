## 1. Brand Persona & Voice Pillars

- **Project Name:** `{{PROJECT_NAME}}`
- **Primary Voice Description:** `{{BRAND_TONE}}`

### The 4 Pillars of Our Voice:
1. **Authoritative, not arrogant:** We speak from firsthand domain competence and verifiable data, without condescension.
2. **Actionable, not academic:** We prioritize clear steps, diagrams, and concrete takeaways over abstract theory.
3. **Punchy, not superficial:** Sentences are concise, active, and direct, but never at the expense of substantive depth.
4. **Empathetic, not melodramatic:** We understand practitioner frustrations intimately and offer pragmatic relief.

| Dimension | We Are | We Are NOT |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clarity** | Direct, unambiguous, and plainspoken | Cryptic, overly jargon-heavy, or vague |

## 3. Vocabulary & Writing Rules

### Preferred vs. Forbidden Terminology:
- **Do NOT Say:** "Revolutionary", "Cutting-edge game-changer", "Synergistic paradigm shift".
- **DO Say:** "Effective", "Production-grade", "Streamlined", "Standardized".
- **Do NOT Say:** "Simply do X" or "Obviously" (dismisses user learning curves).
- **DO Say:** "To accomplish X, execute the following steps".

### Formatting & Readability Rules:
- **Sentence Length:** Aim for an average of 15–20 words per sentence. Vary rhythm with short sentences.
- **Paragraph Length:** Cap paragraphs at 3–4 sentences. Avoid unbroken blocks of text.
- **Visual Scannability:** Use bulleted lists, comparative tables, and bold lead-ins for key insights.
- **Active Voice:** Write in active voice (e.g. "The system parses inputs" instead of "Inputs are parsed by the system").
