## 3. Qualification Framework (BANT Standard)

- **Budget:** Does the prospect have budget allocated to achieve `{{SALES_TARGET}}`?
- **Authority:** Are we in direct contact with the decision owner or economic buyer?
- **Need:** Does their pain point map directly to our solution?
- **Timeline:** Can they deploy or close within our target campaign window?
