## 3. Financial Discipline & Inventory Reconciliation

- [ ] Margin-based price formulation: Net unit cost + target gross margin (30-50%) = Official retail price.
- [ ] End-of-day register balancing (physical cash drawer counted and reconciled against POS register logs).
- [ ] Weekly cycle counts on top 20% fast-moving inventory items.
- [ ] Strict segregation between business operating funds and personal owner accounts.
