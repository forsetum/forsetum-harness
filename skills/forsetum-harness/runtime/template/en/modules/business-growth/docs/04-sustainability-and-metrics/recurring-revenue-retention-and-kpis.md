| Parameter | Instantiation Value |

## Required Decisions

- Define the recurring value the customer receives and how it is observed.
- Select retention and renewal signals before declaring a healthy recurring model.
- Track revenue, gross margin, capacity, churn risk, and service quality together.
- Identify which decisions still depend on the founder and what can be delegated safely.

## Outputs

- A KPI dictionary with owner, frequency, and evidence source.
- A renewal-risk review cadence.
- Capacity and margin thresholds that trigger corrective action.
- A hypothesis-versus-validated-result log.
