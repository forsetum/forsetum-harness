# Positioning and Market Focus — {{PROJECT_NAME}}

> Founder-level decision brief for choosing a focused customer, problem, and market position without presenting hypotheses as validated results.

## Decision Inputs

| Parameter | Instantiation Value |
|---|---|
| Target customer | `{{TARGET_CUSTOMER}}` |
| Core problem | `{{CORE_PROBLEM}}` |
| Business mission | `{{PROJECT_MISSION}}` |
| Decision owner | `{{DECISION_OWNER}}` |

## Required Decisions

- Define the customer segment that has the clearest urgent problem and buying authority.
- State the problem in observable terms, including its current workaround and cost.
- Separate assumptions, evidence, and unresolved questions.
- Choose one primary positioning statement before expanding the offer surface.

## Outputs

- A one-sentence positioning statement.
- A prioritized customer/problem hypothesis.
- Evidence to collect before treating the hypothesis as validated.
- Explicit exclusions that prevent uncontrolled scope expansion.
