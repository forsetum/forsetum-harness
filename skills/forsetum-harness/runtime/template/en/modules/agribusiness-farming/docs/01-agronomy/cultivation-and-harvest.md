## 2. Agronomic Calendar & Integrated Pest Management

1. **Phased Lifecycle Planning:**
   - Seedling / Germination nursery phase (Days 1 to 14).
   - Vegetative stage with calibrated fertilizer fertigation or livestock ration management.
   - Maturation and flowering stage with strict monitoring for crop stress or livestock disease.
2. **Integrated Pest Management (IPM):**
   - Population monitoring against economic damage thresholds prior to chemical or biological intervention.
   - Enforced pre-harvest waiting periods (PHI) to guarantee residue compliance for offtaker markets.
