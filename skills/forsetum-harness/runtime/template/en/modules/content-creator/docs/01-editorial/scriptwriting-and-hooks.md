## 2. Scriptwriting Architecture: Hook — Story — Offer

1. **Hook Window (0 - 3 Seconds):**
   - High-impact pattern interrupt, controversial technical challenge, or visual contrast.
   - Zero slow intro logos or generic conversational greetings.
2. **Body & Story (3 Seconds - 80% Mark):**
   - Concrete code demo, teardown, or practical evidence.
   - Dynamic pacing with camera angle or visual element shifts every 3-5 seconds to optimize audience retention graphs.
3. **Offer & CTA (Final 20%):**
   - Singular conversion goal: Download repo harness, subscribe for deep dives, or join developer community.

## 3. Production Quality Gate

- [ ] Video title and 3 distinct thumbnail compositions designed prior to shoot.
- [ ] First 30 seconds scripted word-for-word and pace-tested.
- [ ] Audio mastering and sound design accents mapped to key takeaways.
- [ ] UTM tracking parameters and conversion links configured in descriptions.
