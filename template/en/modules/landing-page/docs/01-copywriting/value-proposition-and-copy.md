## 2. Copy Hierarchy & Section Content

### 2.1. Hero Section (Above the Fold)
- **Badge / Eyebrow:** Brief credibility or launch indicator (e.g. "Now Available", "Trusted by 500+ Teams").
- **Primary Headline (H1):** Bold, benefit-driven hook answering: *What is this and why does it matter?*
- **Subheadline:** 1–2 sentences expanding on the mechanism and transformation.
- **Hero CTA Button:** Primary action label (`{{PRIMARY_CTA}}`).
- **Friction Reducer:** Micro-copy below CTA (e.g. "No credit card required", "Instant setup in 2 minutes").

### 2.2. Social Proof & Authority
- Logos of trusted partners, customer logos, or industry badges.
- Metric counter (e.g. "Over 10,000 requests processed").

### 2.3. Problem vs. Solution
- **The Old Way (Pain Points):** 3 friction points the user currently faces.
- **The New Way (With {{PROJECT_NAME}}):** How `{{PROJECT_NAME}}` eliminates that friction.

### 2.4. Key Features & Benefits (3 Pillars)
1. **Feature 1:** Benefit-driven headline, descriptive paragraph, and visual asset reference.
2. **Feature 2:** Benefit-driven headline, descriptive paragraph, and visual asset reference.
3. **Feature 3:** Benefit-driven headline, descriptive paragraph, and visual asset reference.

### 2.5. Testimonials & Social Proof
- 2–3 quoted reviews with customer name, title, company, and avatar.

### 2.6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
- 4–6 clear Q&A items addressing common objections (pricing, onboarding, compatibility, support).

### 2.7. Closing CTA Section
- High-urgency summary headline reiterating the core transformation.
- Secondary instance of `{{PRIMARY_CTA}}`.
