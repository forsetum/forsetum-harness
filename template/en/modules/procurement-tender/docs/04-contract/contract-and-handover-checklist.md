## 4. Dispute Resolution Mechanism

Disputes arising from contract execution are resolved in escalating stages:

| Stage | Mechanism | Timeframe | Parties Involved | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Stage 1** | Amicable Settlement | 14 calendar days | Owner & Provider | Written mutual agreement |
| **Stage 2** | Mediation | 30 calendar days | Owner, Provider, Independent Mediator | Binding settlement agreement |
| **Stage 3** | Arbitration / Litigation | Per regulations | National Arbitration Board or District Court | Final and binding decision |

> During the dispute resolution process, the Provider remains obligated to continue work execution unless otherwise instructed in writing by the Commitment Officer.
