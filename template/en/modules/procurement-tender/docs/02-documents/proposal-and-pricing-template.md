# Proposal Document & Cost Budget Template — {{PROJECT_NAME}}

> Standard format for the official bid letter, Cost Budget Plan (Bill of Quantities), supporting document checklist, and submission schedule for `{{PROJECT_NAME}}` procurement process.

---

## 1. Official Bid Letter

**Letter Format:**

```
[COMPANY LETTERHEAD]

Reference : [Reference Number]
Attachment: [Number of Attachments]
Subject   : Bid for {{PROCUREMENT_TYPE}} — {{PROJECT_NAME}}

To:
{{DECISION_OWNER}}
[Address of Commitment Officer / Procurement Committee]

Dear Sir/Madam,

In response to the tender announcement [Announcement Number] dated
[Date], we hereby submit our bid for the following work:

Work Title       : {{PROJECT_NAME}}
Procurement Type : {{PROCUREMENT_TYPE}}
Bid Amount       : [Currency] [Amount in Figures] ([Amount in Words])
Validity Period  : [XX] calendar days from the date of this letter

We appreciate the opportunity and look forward to your favorable
consideration.

[City], [Date]
[Company Name]

[Signature & Stamp]
[Name of Director / Principal]
[Title]
```
