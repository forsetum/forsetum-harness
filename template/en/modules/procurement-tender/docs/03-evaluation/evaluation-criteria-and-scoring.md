## 3. Price Evaluation Method

The price evaluation method is aligned with `{{PROCUREMENT_METHOD}}`:

### Option A: Merit Point System (Quality & Price)
- Combined weight: **Technical *(X%)*** + **Price *(Y%)***.
- Price score formula: `(Owner's Estimate or Lowest Price / Bid Price) × Price Weight × 100`.
- Winner: Bidder with the **highest combined total score**.

### Option B: Lowest Evaluated Price (Pass/Fail System)
- Technical evaluation is pass/fail (meets / does not meet minimum threshold).
- Winner: Bidder that **passes technical evaluation** with the **lowest evaluated bid price** not exceeding the owner's estimate.

### Option C: Life Cycle Cost
- Evaluation considers total cost of ownership (TCO) over the useful life, including acquisition, operational, maintenance, and disposal costs.
- Winner: Bidder with the **lowest TCO** meeting the technical threshold.

## 4. Clarification & Negotiation Procedures

### 4.1. Technical Clarification
- The committee reserves the right to request written clarification on ambiguous or unclear items in bid documents.
- Clarifications must not alter the substance of the bid (price, key specifications, or key personnel).
- Clarification response deadline: ***(X) working days*** from the date of request.

### 4.2. Technical & Price Negotiation
- Negotiation is conducted **only with the top-ranked bidder** from the combined evaluation.
- Negotiation scope: technical optimization, volume/specification adjustments, and unit price reasonableness.
- Negotiation results are documented in a Negotiation Minutes signed by both parties.

### 4.3. Winner Determination & Announcement
- The winner is determined by the Commitment Officer based on the Procurement Committee's recommendation.
- Announcement is made through the official portal and direct notification to bidders.
- Objection period: ***(X) working days*** from the official announcement.
- Objection resolution follows the mechanism prescribed by `{{REGULATORY_FRAMEWORK}}`.
