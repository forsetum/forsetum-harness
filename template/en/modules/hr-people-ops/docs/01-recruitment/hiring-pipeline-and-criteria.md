## 2. End-to-End Hiring Pipeline

The recruitment process operates systematically from new headcount requisition to official job offering:

| Stage | Activity Name | Description & Output | Responsible Role | Target SLA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Job Requisition & JD | Headcount request submission and Job Description sign-off | Hiring Manager & HR | 3 business days |
| **2** | Sourcing & Job Posting | Job broadcast across career portals, professional networks, and social media | HR Talent Acquisition | 7–14 business days |
| **3** | Resume & Portfolio Screening | Candidate filtering based on baseline qualifications and role alignment | HR Talent Acquisition | 2–3 days per batch |
| **4** | Initial HR Interview | Exploratory screen covering culture fit, motivation, availability, and compensation band | HR Talent Acquisition | 30 mins per candidate |
| **5** | Technical Assessment | Practical competence test, coding challenge, case study, or role assignment | Technical Team / User | 3–5 business days |
| **6** | User & Stakeholder Interview | In-depth interview with departmental leadership assessing functional depth & culture add | Hiring Lead / Dept Head | 45–60 mins per candidate |
| **7** | Reference & Background Check | Verification of employment history, credentials, and professional reputation | HR Talent Acquisition | 2–3 business days |
| **8** | Offering & Agreement | Formal Offer Letter issuance, compensation agreement, and start-date confirmation | HR & {{DECISION_OWNER}} | 3 business days |

## 4. Selection Criteria & Interview Scorecard

Candidate evaluation throughout the interview rounds relies on a quantitative 1–5 scale rubric:

| Evaluation Dimension | Specific Focus | Weight | Score (1–5) | Evaluation Notes / Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Technical Competence** | Depth of functional knowledge and case study execution | 30% | [ ] | *(Demonstrated strengths and skill gaps)* |
| **Relevant Experience** | Past project track record and solving comparable problems | 25% | [ ] | *(Industry relevance and system scale)* |
| **Problem Solving** | Structured approach to decomposing ambiguity and decision making | 20% | [ ] | *(Logical reasoning and analytical depth)* |
| **Culture & Values** | Team alignment, workplace ethics, transparency, and integrity | 15% | [ ] | *(Team compatibility and workplace behaviors)* |
| **Drive & Growth Mindset** | Willingness to learn, curiosity, and alignment with organizational goals | 10% | [ ] | *(Long-term ambition and adaptability)* |
| **Weighted Total Score** | | **100%** | **[Total]** | **Recommendation: Strong Hire / Hire / Reconsider / Reject** |

> **Scoring Scale Guidelines:**
> **1 (Unsatisfactory):** Fails to meet foundational role requirements.
> **2 (Marginal):** Meets minor requirements with critical gaps.
> **3 (Competent):** Meets expected standard criteria solidly.
> **4 (Strong):** Exceeds role expectations across key aspects.
> **5 (Exceptional):** Outstanding mastery; sets a benchmark for the team.
