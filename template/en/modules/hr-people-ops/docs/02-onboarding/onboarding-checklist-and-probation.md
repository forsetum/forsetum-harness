## 2. Early-Stage Onboarding Checklist

### 2.1. Pre-boarding (Day -7 to Day -1)
- [ ] Send welcome letter and first-day logistics to personal email.
- [ ] Provision corporate email, internal system access, and team chat channels.
- [ ] Prepare workstation hardware (laptop, peripherals, software licenses, office access).
- [ ] Appoint and brief dedicated onboarding buddy.

### 2.2. Day One (Day 1)
- [ ] Welcome reception, equipment handover, and physical/virtual office tour.
- [ ] Employee handbook and compliance guidelines briefing per `{{COMPLIANCE_STANDARD}}`.
- [ ] Complete statutory HR documentation (ID, tax compliance, payroll banking, benefits).
- [ ] Introduction to team members and dedicated onboarding buddy.
- [ ] Kick-off alignment meeting with direct manager for role overview.

### 2.3. Week One (Week 1)
- [ ] Comprehensive access to divisional documentation, technical guides, and standard SOPs.
- [ ] Cross-functional orientation sessions covering the business context and product mission of `{{PROJECT_NAME}}`.
- [ ] Finalize 30-60-90 day performance goals and success metrics.
- [ ] Conduct end-of-week 1-on-1 check-in with direct supervisor.

## 3. 30-60-90 Day Execution Plan

| Timeline | Core Focus | Target Deliverables | Success Measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Month 1 (Days 1–30)**: *Learn & Assimilate* | Deep dive into systems, internal team workflows, and key stakeholder mapping | Complete onboarding curriculum, shadow core operational loops, deliver minor task | Navigates core operational workflows independently |
| **Month 2 (Days 31–60)**: *Execute & Collaborate* | Independent task execution and cross-team collaboration | Own medium-complexity initiatives, participate in reviews and issue resolutions | Delivers primary job outputs meeting quality standards and SLAs |
| **Month 3 (Days 61–90)**: *Contribute & Lead* | Full role autonomy and operational enhancement proposals | Ship quarterly deliverables autonomously, propose process improvement initiative | Demonstrates full competence and autonomous ownership of role |

---

## 4. Onboarding Buddy System Guidelines

1. **Buddy Role Definition:** A peer colleague (outside the direct reporting chain) assigned to assist the new hire with social assimilation and daily operational navigation during their initial 3 months.
2. **Cadence:**
   - **Week 1:** Daily 15-minute informal check-in (Q&A, workflow friction removal).
   - **Weeks 2–4:** Bi-weekly check-ins.
   - **Months 2–3:** Weekly or ad-hoc informal coffee chats.
3. **Focus Areas:** Navigating team culture, tool best practices, and informal organizational practices.
