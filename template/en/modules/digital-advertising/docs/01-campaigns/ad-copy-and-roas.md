## 2. Paid Funnel Architecture

1. **Top of Funnel (TOF) — Cold Audience Awareness:**
   - Broad algorithmic targeting or lookalikes to capture fresh prospects.
   - Core angles: Core pain points, architectural myths, and innovative protocol benefits.
2. **Middle of Funnel (MOF) — Proof & Consideration:**
   - Retargeting 30-day site visitors or 50%+ video viewers.
   - Core angles: Concrete case studies, verifiable benchmarks, and developer testimonials.
3. **Bottom of Funnel (BOF) — Direct Conversion:**
   - Retargeting abandoned signups and cart sessions.
   - Core angles: Limited-time onboarding guarantees, direct CTA, and immediate activation links.
