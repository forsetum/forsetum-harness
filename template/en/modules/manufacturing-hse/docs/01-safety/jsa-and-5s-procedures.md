## 2. Job Safety Analysis (JSA) & Hazard Control Hierarchy

1. **Station-Level Job Safety Analysis (JSA):**
   - Granular identification of mechanical, thermal, electrical, and ergonomic risks for each workstation task.
2. **Hierarchy of Hazard Controls:**
   - **Elimination & Substitution:** Removing hazard sources or substituting hazardous materials.
   - **Engineering Controls:** Machine interlocks, emergency stop cables, and localized exhaust ventilation.
   - **Administrative Controls:** Standard operating procedures, operator rotation, and high-visibility floor markings.
   - **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Safety eyewear, high-impact hardhats, heat-resistant gloves, steel-toed footwear.
