# Deliverables & Milestone Artifacts Specification — {{PROJECT_NAME}}

> Formatting rules, document templates, and acceptance criteria for outputs produced under `{{PROJECT_NAME}}`.

---

## 1. Primary Deliverable Specification

- **Deliverable Title:** `{{PRIMARY_DELIVERABLE}}`
- **Standard File Formats:** PDF (Formal reports), Markdown/DOCX (Drafts), XLSX/CSV (Data summaries).
- **Target Audience:** Executive leadership and project stakeholders.

---

## 2. Standard Document Structure

Every formal document or report produced must follow this structure:

1. **Cover / Metadata Header:** Document title, author, date, version, and sign-off status.
2. **Executive Summary:** Max 1 page summarizing purpose, key findings, and recommended actions.
3. **Methodology & Source Data:** Transparent explanation of data inputs and analysis steps.
4. **Detailed Findings / Deliverable Body:** Sectioned analysis, data tables, or procedural records.
5. **Action Items & Next Steps:** Assigned owners and deadlines for pending decisions.
6. **Appendices:** Raw data, source links, and meeting minutes.

---

## 3. Formatting & Style Rules

- **Naming Convention:** `YYYY-MM-DD_<project-code>_<deliverable-name>_v<version>.<ext>`
- **Tone of Voice:** Professional, objective, data-backed, and unambiguous.
- **Data Integrity:** Numerical totals must reconcile with source sheets.
