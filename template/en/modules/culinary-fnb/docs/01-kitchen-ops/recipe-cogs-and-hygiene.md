## 3. HACCP Sanitation & Daily Food Waste Tracking

- [ ] Daily opening prep and closing sanitation checklists countersigned by shift sous chef.
- [ ] Chiller (1-4°C) and deep freezer (-18°C) temperature monitoring logs audited twice daily.
- [ ] Shift kitchen waste sheets tracking expired, burned, or dropped ingredients actively maintained.
- [ ] FIFO (First In First Out) rotation enforced on all perishable and dry stock with explicit color-coded date tags.
