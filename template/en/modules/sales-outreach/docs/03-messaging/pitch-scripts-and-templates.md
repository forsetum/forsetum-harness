## 1. Multi-Channel Touchpoint Sequence

### Touch 1: Initial Cold Outreach (Day 1)
- **Subject:** Quick question regarding {{RELEVANT_TOPIC}}
- **Hook:** Reference a specific observation or relevant trigger event.
- **Problem Statement:** 1 sentence highlighting common inefficiency faced by `{{ICP_PROFILE}}`.
- **Value Hook:** How `{{PROJECT_NAME}}` solves it.
- **Low-Friction Call-to-Action:** "Worth a 5-minute chat this Thursday, or should I send a 2-minute overview first?"

### Touch 2: Value-Add Follow-up (Day 4)
- Share a case study, benchmark data, or practical tip relevant to their industry.

### Touch 3: Social / Channel Pivot (Day 8)
- Reach out via secondary channel in `{{OUTREACH_CHANNELS}}` referencing previous message.

### Touch 4: The Clean Breakup (Day 14)
- Polite withdrawal message letting the prospect know you won't clutter their inbox, keeping door open.

## 2. Objection Handling Scripts

| Objection | Core Concern | Recommended Response Script |
|---|---|---|
| *"We already have a solution."* | Reluctance to change | *"Totally understand. Most of our clients used [Competitor] before seeing our 30% faster workflow. Mind if I share a 1-page comparison for future reference?"* |
| *"We don't have budget right now."* | Cost vs value | *"Understood. Many teams start with our pilot program which pays for itself within 30 days. Would you be open to seeing how the numbers work?"* |
| *"Send me an email with info."* | Brush-off | *"Gladly! To make sure I send only what's relevant to your team, are you currently more focused on [Pain A] or [Pain B]?"* |
