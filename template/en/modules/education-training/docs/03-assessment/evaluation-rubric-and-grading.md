## 3. Capstone Evaluation Rubric (1–4 Scale)

Practical projects and portfolio submissions are scored using an analytic 4-tier rubric:

| Evaluation Dimension | Weight | 1 - Needs Support (<65) | 2 - Developing (65–74) | 3 - Proficient (75–89) | 4 - Exemplary (90–100) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Conceptual Application** | 35% | Flawed solution logic; fails foundational requirements | Partially functional; core logical gaps observed | Solid execution; satisfies all stated requirements | Elegant, resilient implementation exceeding specs |
| **Technical Architecture** | 25% | Disorganized codebase; industry conventions neglected | Basic structure present but exhibits bad practices | Adheres cleanly to architectural patterns and standards | Highly modular, clean architecture with excellent maintainability |
| **Documentation & Tests** | 20% | Missing documentation and tests entirely | Minimal documentation; tests only cover happy paths | Clear documentation; solid coverage of core paths | Comprehensive docs; rigorous edge-case test coverage |
| **Presentation & Defense** | 20% | Unable to articulate rationales behind decisions | Hesitant responses; difficulty defending trade-offs | Communicates technical flow clearly and systematically | Highly articulate, persuasive; commands domain mastery |

---

## 4. Remediation & Late Submission Policy

1. **Remedial Eligibility:** Learners achieving a cumulative score between 60–74 qualify for one remediation round or project revision within 7 calendar days.
2. **Remedial Grade Cap:** Re-assessed submissions are capped at the baseline passing grade (75 / B).
3. **Late Policy:** Submissions incur a 10% penalty per calendar day late, up to a maximum cutoff of 3 days.

## 5. Certification Structure & Honors

Learners satisfying all academic criteria receive an official credential:

### 5.1. Honors Classification
- **Score 90 – 100:** Passed with Distinction
- **Score 80 – 89:** Passed with Merit
- **Score 75 – 79:** Passed
- **Score < 75:** Certificate of Participation
